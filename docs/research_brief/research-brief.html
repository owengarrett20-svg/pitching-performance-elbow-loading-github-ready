Pitching Performance
and Elbow Loading

Exploratory analysis of Driveline’s OpenBiomechanics Project · independent project, not a peer-reviewed publication.

411recorded pitches
100athletes
2–5pitches per athlete

Research question. Do completed-pitch rotational summaries add predictive information beyond age, body mass, and height for pitch speed and peak internal elbow varus moment in held-out athletes?

Design and data trust

One-to-one source pitch join; 100 sessions. Missing focal values: 0; 25 other POI columns contain missing observations. Missing outcomes remain missing.

5 shared athlete-grouped folds (seed 42); four fixed baselines; ridge alpha 10. Training-only median imputation, missingness indicators, and standardization. Predictors are age/mass/height, three rotational peaks, or all six. Outcomes, IDs, and filenames are excluded.

The comparison plan followed viewed Phase 1 results. This is exploratory, not preregistered or independently confirmatory. Predictors describe a completed pitch, not a pre-pitch forecast.

Increment remains uncertain: interval includes zero.

Intervals use 5,000 paired athlete resamples of fixed out-of-fold errors and exclude full model-refitting uncertainty. The 10 overlapping refitted partitions are descriptive sensitivity checks, not independent replications or confidence intervals.

The source cohort is primarily collegiate and each athlete has one released assessment. These results do not establish external performance, longitudinal development, causal coaching effects, or injury risk.
