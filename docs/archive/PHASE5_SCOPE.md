# Phase 5: source-linked pitch exploration and a defensible research portfolio

This release extends published Phase 4 at `ad4cd71`. Its purpose is to connect
an observed pitch to its available source motion, markers, measurements, and
research evidence, and to make the work accurately describable in a PhD
application. It introduces no new predictive model or external-validation claim.

## Source-linked exploration

The default pitch view will offer literal search, a selectable recorded-pitch
table, accessible selection fallbacks, and two clearly identified comparison
panes. Selecting a row must update the displayed pitch, available marker replay,
associated measurements, and shareable location together. A stale row position,
empty search, or excluded pitch must not be silently substituted with another
pitch's motion.

Prepared motion may expand using the cached, checksum-verified official C3D
archive, matched to source pitch and athlete identities. Coverage is determined
from actual source files. These are measured-marker replays; camera recordings,
anatomical joint-center validation, common event alignment, and complete signal
coverage are not implied. The existing three detailed trace/MP4 examples remain
available with their original provenance.

## Evidence and application materials

The research brief will derive cohort counts and model results from verified
saved reports. It will distinguish software verification and source-scalar
reproduction from internal held-out evaluation, independent biomechanical
validation, and an unperformed external study. The four released scalar peaks
in the three existing trace examples form twelve explicit reproduction checks;
matching these peaks does not validate inverse dynamics or marker–signal timing.

The PhD materials will describe an independent AI-assisted research software
project, not a publication or an established clinical/sports-team tool. Confirmed
personal contributions include project framing, requirements, deployment,
complete interface review, and iterative interaction design. AI assistance
provided substantial implementation, tests, method drafts, and agent-executed
checks. Personal code-level replication or independent calculations will not be
attributed without confirmation.

CV-specific narratives and the uploaded CV remain outside the public repository.
Public guidance describes contribution boundaries and a general application
process. Program rules, current faculty availability, and admissions outcomes
are not inferred from project features or another assistant's earlier advice.

## Invariants and acceptance

- Preserve source data, the Phase 2 plan, fixed models, grouped evaluations,
  conditional interval scope, null/uncertain findings, and source licenses.
- Keep private study/application files separate from public exports.
- Missing values remain missing; measurement units and source identities remain
  inspectable. Marker display transformations do not compute a net joint moment.
- Test search and selection integrity, both comparison identities, unavailable
  recordings, corrupt assets, source/brief checks, and offline hosted behavior.
- Check real desktop/phone interactions, report printing, a clean-source science
  reproduction, and the Windows bundle upgrade from the published base.

The next scientific phase requires independently authorized external data or a
separately justified measurement/timing validation study. A stronger interface
and application narrative do not complete that research.
